description: "Helena Rong (Assistant Professor at NYU Shanghai, director of the Protopolis Lab) presents a taxonomy of 'trust experience glitches'—six failure modes (agency assertion, epistemic camouflage, delegation ambiguity, opacity at scale, cascading autonomy, revocation failure) drawn from a 167-incident dataset of agentic AI failures. She argues these ruptures expose a 'consequence reception problem' where autonomous agents lack a stable body to bear accountability, and proposes that governance must shift from ex-post legal remedies toward upstream protocol primitives (capability-based permissions, provenance tracking, action-class legibility, circuit breakers, revocability) as the 'traffic lights' of the agentic web.</summary>"
