description: "Patrick Atwater, innovation program manager at the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California, argues that protocols exist simultaneously as documented formalisms and undocumented implicit norms, using case studies from a neighborhood park, bridge/infrastructure permitting, and track-2 diplomacy to illustrate this gap. He proposes practical strategies for navigating illegible terrain—black-boxing unknowns, cultivating domain-expert practitioners as guides, and lightweight fieldwork logging (e.g., a 'protocol watching' Notion database)—and shares a real example of a water utility valve project where jurisdictional coordination revealed pseudo-official knowledge and unwritten norms."
