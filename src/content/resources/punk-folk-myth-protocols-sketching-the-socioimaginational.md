description: "Sam Chua presents a framework distinguishing 'punk' (technology without technocracy) as a recurring pattern in Southeast Asian history—sea-punk trade networks, rice-punk irrigation governance like Bali's subak system, and forest-punk hill refuges—arguing these predate and parallel modern cyberpunk sensibilities. He introduces the 'social imaginational' as a distinct layer from the sociological, governed by dream-like, caricature-style logics (metaphor, myth, shadow-puppet non-seriousness) that resist reduction into protocols, and proposes a punk-prep protocol cycle (dangerous protocols breed punks, who build good protocols, which breed complacent 'prep' protocols, which decay back into dangerous ones) as a lens for protocol literacy and design."
