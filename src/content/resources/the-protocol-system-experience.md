description: "Angela Walch, a former law professor and Summer of Protocols researcher, presents her framework 'The Protocol System Experience,' which maps an individual's (personified as 'Pip') journey through any protocol system—from blockchains to families to religions—using concepts like well-being (thriving vs. dysphoria), fit, and autonomy. She argues that autonomy requires both awareness (seeing the 'water' you swim in) and insight (deep knowledge of power dynamics), and introduces terms like 'protocol determinism,' 'protocol overhang,' and 'protocol scars' to describe how membership in one system compels or haunts behavior in others."
