description: "Yuhan Liu (Princeton PhD student, presenting work with Varun Nagaraj Rao, Sohyeon Hwang, Janet Vertesi, and Andrés Monroy-Hernández) presents OpenCourier, a protocol-based alternative to centralized delivery platforms like DoorDash and Uber Eats, built after mapping 495 'indie' delivery platforms that share homogenous but siloed software. The architecture uses registries, app-instance layers, and instance-requester communication (inspired by ActivityPub/Fediverse) to let independently-run courier instances interoperate, giving workers like a hypothetical 'Bob' the ability to move across platforms with portable reputation data while enforcing transparency requirements such as mandatory deactivation reasons."
